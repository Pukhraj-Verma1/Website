About the clinic

Neighbourhood healthcare, delivered with dignity

Karuna Clinic is a modern general medical practice in Shivaji Nagar, Sector 11, Gurugram. The name Karuna means compassion — and that principle shapes how we listen, diagnose and treat.

We keep things simple: unhurried consultations, clear explanations of your condition, evidence-based treatment and only the tests you genuinely need. Whether it's a fever at short notice or ongoing management of diabetes or blood pressure, you'll always be seen by a doctor who knows your history.

  • Walk-ins and prior appointments both welcome
  • Sterile, well-maintained treatment room
  • Easy follow-up on call or WhatsApp

Our services

Comprehensive care under one roof

Everyday medical needs for adults and children, handled thoroughly and affordably.

General Medicine & OPD

Diagnosis and treatment of fever, infections, respiratory issues, gastric complaints and seasonal illnesses.

Diabetes & BP Management

Structured long-term monitoring, medication review, diet counselling and periodic health check-ups.

Minor Procedures & Dressing

Wound care, dressings, suturing, injections, nebulisation and other minor in-clinic procedures.

Child & Adult Vaccination

Routine immunisation schedules, flu shots and travel vaccines administered under proper cold-chain care.

Diagnostics & Lab Tests

Sample collection and reporting for routine blood work, sugar profiles and preventive health packages.

Health Counselling

Lifestyle, nutrition and preventive guidance, plus specialist referrals when advanced care is required.
